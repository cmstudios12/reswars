What happens when I click Purchase ticket?

Choose your event, ticket category and quantity, then enter your details. During testing, you receive a confirmation email and a sample PDF. No online payment is collected. Only staff can approve payment and issue valid entry tickets.

How do payments work during testing?

Yoco is temporarily disabled for this test. You will not be charged online. Your booking is saved, and My Angel staff will manage payment confirmation separately.

When will I receive my QR ticket?

After our staff confirms receipt, your individual QR entry tickets are emailed as a PDF attachment. You can also access them on the booking status page.

Where is the lodge located?

My Angel Country Lodge is in Mjejane, Nkomazi, Mpumalanga. Each event page displays the venue, time and a directions link.

Who do I contact if there's a booking issue?

Contact the My Angel ticket team on WhatsApp at +27 79 868 2198. Include your booking reference so we can help you.
